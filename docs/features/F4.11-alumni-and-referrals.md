# F4.11 — Alumni and referrals

**Status:** ✅ Built · ✅ Deployed · ✅ Unit tested · ✅ End-to-end checked (2026-10-06)

## Referral codes

Every family member or alumnus can have a personal **referral code** (`Account.Referral_Code__c`, e.g. `SHA-7Q2X`: three letters of the surname and four characters without look-alikes such as 0/O or 1/I/L). Codes are created on request: by the family in the portal (**Refer a friend** → **Get my referral code**), by staff, or when an alumnus is invited.

## Tracking

1. **Enquiry:** a code given on an enquiry (staff entry, website form, or the enquiry API field `referralCode`) is tidied (case, spaces), matched to its owner, the enquiry gets **Referred By** and channel _Referral_, and a **Referral** (REF-000001) is recorded as _Enquired_. Unknown codes are kept on the enquiry but create nothing.
2. **Enrolment:** when the converted learner enrols for the first time, the referral becomes _Enrolled_ with the enrolment, learner and date.
3. **Reward:** a credit note (origin _Referral_) of `Referral_Reward_Amount` (default 500) for the referrer, used automatically on their next invoice, and the _Referral Reward_ message. `Referral_Reward_Mode`: **Manual** (default; finance grants it on the desk), **Auto** (granted when the referred learner enrols) or **Off**. A referral is rewarded once; finance can mark one **Not eligible** with a note.

## Alumni

A learner whose enrolment is **Completed** and who has no other active enrolment becomes an **alumnus** (`Alumni__c`, `Alumni_Since__c`). `Alumni_Contact_Ok__c` (ticked by default) records whether they want news; **Invite** on the desk sends the _Alumni Invite_ message (staff text plus the alumnus's referral code) to the alumni who allow contact.

## Referrals and alumni desk (`kemReferrals`)

On the Home page and each Branch page: counts (referred enquiries, enrolled with reward due, rewarded), the share of referrals that enrolled, alumni total, the reward rule; the latest referrals with **Grant reward** / **Not eligible**; the top five referrers (joined of referred, code); alumni who allow contact with **Invite selected**.

## Portal

The **Refer a friend** card shows the family's code (or a button to create it), the reward if rewards are on, and how many they referred and how many joined.

## Access

Administrators and branch managers see and manage referrals; admissions counsellors see referrals and invite alumni; granting rewards and marking not eligible need **KEM Manage Billing** (finance, administrators). Referral codes are matched in system mode so any enquiry channel can use them.
