## `Phase4JourneyTest`

At a new centre, **Phase Four Centre**, with a class, a bus route and a family who already uses Kasetti:

1. **A referred family is answered, enrolled and billed for transport:** an enquiry with the family's referral code in lower case is linked to the referrer (channel _Referral_, referral _Enquired_); Einstein (a test stand-in) drafts the reply, the counsellor edits and records it (outcome _Edited_); a policy question is answered with its source; conversion and enrolment make the referral _Enrolled_; finance grants the reward (credit note with origin _Referral_); the learner is put on the route from today and one invoice carries tuition and transport.
2. **Learners sit an exam:** two enrolled learners are registered, found eligible, seated and issued hall tickets (two PDFs on the candidates); marks are entered, saved and published to the gradebook; the exam becomes _Results Published_.
3. **The forecast is explained and the benefit is measured:** the branch forecast has enrolment and finance figures; Einstein explains it; the explanation is reviewed (rating 5); the AI console counts the request, the minutes saved and one AI user.

Access is part of the test: the AI steps run as an administrator with **KEM AI User**; without it, the assistants refuse (`AiServiceTest`).
