# F4.2–F4.5 — AI assistants: enquiry replies, progress and report comments, record summaries, policy Q&A

**Status:** ✅ Built · ✅ Deployed · ✅ Unit tested · ✅ End-to-end checked (2026-10-06)

All assistants follow [F4.1](F4.1-ai-foundation.md): Einstein drafts from live data, staff decide, every use is logged and measured. Panels show only to users with **KEM AI User** while the feature is switched on.

## F4.2 Enquiry reply drafts (`kemAiEnquiryReply`, enquiry page)

**Draft reply** builds an email to the family from the enquiry (names, learner age, channel, delivery preference, notes), the course asked about, **current prices** (branch price first, then the organisation-wide price) and up to three **upcoming classes** with their weekly times, plus an optional counsellor note (for example "offer a free trial on Saturday"). The prompt forbids inventing prices, dates or promises and asks for plain text signed by the counsellor.

The draft opens in an editable box with a star rating and **Discard**, **Regenerate** and **Use reply**. Use reply logs a completed task "Email reply (AI-assisted)" with the final text on the enquiry and sets Last Contacted (the counsellor sends the email from their own mailbox, as Email Delivery is Off). The interaction is marked Accepted or Edited by the share of words changed; Discard marks it Rejected.

## F4.3 Learner progress summaries and report-card comments

- **Learner progress** (`kemAiProgress`, learner page): a short, constructive summary for the family from the learner's classes, attendance, grades and recent published results, with one area to focus on and a suggestion for home. Staff edit it and **Send to family** sends it as a message to the fee payer (portal; email when delivery is on) through the normal messaging rules.
- **Report-card comments** (`kemAiReportComments`, class page): for each learner of the class, **Draft** proposes a comment of about 70 words from their grade, attendance and results; the teacher edits and **Save** stores it as the report-card comment (`GradingService.saveComments`), so it appears on the report card when grades are finalised.

## F4.4 Record summaries (`kemAiSummary`)

On the enquiry, application, learner, class and branch pages. **Summarise** gives three to six bullets with what matters and the next step:

| Record      | Facts used                                                                                                                          |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enquiry     | Status, course, channel, age of the enquiry, next follow-up, last contact, trial date, notes, possible duplicate, recent activities |
| Application | Status, course, eligibility, checklist items, decision and reason, offer status and expiry                                          |
| Class       | Status, seats, sessions left, learners with attendance and scores                                                                   |
| Branch      | **Daily brief**: today's sessions, cover needed, learners at risk, fees outstanding (finance users), attendance, seats filled       |
| Learner     | Progress summary (as F4.3)                                                                                                          |

Helpful / not helpful buttons record the outcome.

## F4.5 Staff policy Q&A (`kemAiAsk`, Home page)

Staff ask a question in their own words. `AiKnowledge` finds the best matching articles of the institution's knowledge base (`AI_Knowledge__mdt`, 10 articles: fees and invoices, payments, refunds and credit, attendance, waitlists and transfers, admissions and offers, grading and report cards, library, messaging and consent, AI use). Articles quote **live settings** (for example `{{Setting:Default_Invoice_Due_Days}}`), so answers follow the current configuration. Einstein answers **only from those articles** and returns the sources it used; sources not given to it are dropped, and an answer without sources is shown as not confident. A question no article covers gets "I could not find this in the institution's policies" without calling the model.

To add or change a policy: edit or add an `AI_Knowledge__mdt` record (title, body, keywords) and deploy.

## Tests

`AiServiceTest` covers each assistant with a stub model (facts passed to the model, results saved, permission checks). Jest: `kemAiDraft`, `kemAiEnquiryReply`, `kemAiProgress`, `kemAiReportComments`, `kemAiSummary`, `kemAiAsk`.
