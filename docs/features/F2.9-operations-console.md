**Status:** ✅ Built · ✅ Deployed · ✅ Unit tested · ✅ End-to-end checked (2026-10-05)

## Operations console

Kasetti home page → **Operations** (`kemOpsConsole`), shown only to administrators (read and edit access to the error log); everyone else does not see it. `OpsService` answers two questions: is everything that runs on its own running, and what is waiting for a person?

| Section          | Content                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Health           | **All systems normal** (green), **Needs attention** (amber: errors in the last 24 hours, work in error queues) or **Action required** (red: a job not scheduled or a background run failed), with the specific issues listed                         |
| Exception queues | Reconciliation exceptions, refunds awaiting approval, approved refunds to pay out, sessions needing cover, messages failed (7 days), emails queued over an hour, overdue invoices, waitlist offers expiring within 24 hours — each with where to act |
| Scheduled jobs   | Every `KemJobs` schedule (Offer Expiry, Waitlist Offers, Recurring Billing, Payment Reminders, Message Dispatch) with state and next/last run; **Run now** per job; **Schedule missing jobs**                                                        |
| Background runs  | KEM batch, queueable and scheduled runs of the last 7 days with items processed, errors and failure detail                                                                                                                                           |
| Errors           | ERROR/FATAL error-log entries of the last 7 days by source, and the 20 newest                                                                                                                                                                        |

"Run now" starts the same work as the schedule (recurring billing and offer expiry as batches; waitlist offers, payment reminders and message dispatch as a queueable). Run from the console, payment reminders deliver email in the same job instead of starting another one.

## Phase 2 journey (`Phase2JourneyTest`)

One family — guardian Gauri (fee payer, email, portal access) and children Lata and Manu — through every Phase 2 feature:

1. **Waitlist:** Lata fills the one-seat class; Manu joins the waitlist; a seat opens → Manu is offered it and **Gauri is notified**; Manu accepts and is enrolled; the class is full again.
2. **Credit:** Lata's first invoice (admission + first month = 3,540) is overpaid by 500 → reconciliation exception → **held as credit** → **applied automatically** to Manu's first invoice; invoice and payment notices reach Gauri.
3. **Recurring billing and instalments:** next month's invoice (2,360) is raised by the recurring billing logic and split into two instalments; the portal shows both children, the 5,900 due and the instalments.
4. **Cover and grading:** Lata's teacher reports an absence; the cover desk suggests the second teacher, who is assigned (absence Covered); the teacher publishes a quiz (17/20), **finalises grades** and the report card is issued; Gauri sees the **A (Final)** grade and report card in the portal and has the report-card notice.
