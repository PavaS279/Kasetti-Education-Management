# F2.5 — Messaging: templated notifications with consent

**Status:** ✅ Built · ⬜ Deployed · ⬜ Unit tested · ⬜ End-to-end checked

## How it works

`MessageService` turns business events into messages. Every message is a `Message__c` record (MSG-000001): the outbox, the audit trail, and the family's portal inbox.

| Channel | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Portal  | **Delivered** at once; shown in the family portal (F2.8)                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Email   | **Queued**, then sent by a queueable job (`DispatchJob`) as a branded HTML + plain-text email from the branch name. **Suppressed** if the recipient opted out of email or has no address; **Not Sent** while delivery is off; **Failed** with the reason if the platform rejects it |

- **Consent:** the standard _Email Opt Out_ on the guardian or learner. Staff change it from the learner page; the change is made as the user (field and record access apply).
- **No duplicates:** `Dedup_Key__c` (unique: event, channel, recipient, record, plus the due date/reminder day for reminders), so retries and the daily job never send the same notification twice.
- **Never breaks the business action:** notification failures are caught and written to the error log.
- **Go-live switch:** Education Setting `Email_Delivery` is **Off** in this org, so emails are logged as Not Sent and not delivered. Set it to `Live` (Setup → Custom Metadata Types → Education Setting) once the sender address and deliverability are confirmed.

## Templates (`Message_Template__mdt`, editable in Setup)

| Event (record name) | Sent when                                                               | To                                |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Invoice_Issued      | An invoice is issued (manually or by recurring billing)                 | Invoice bill-to                   |
| Payment_Received    | A payment is confirmed (not credit applications)                        | Payer                             |
| Invoice_Due_Soon    | Daily, `Due_Soon_Days` (3) before the due date (next instalment)        | Invoice bill-to                   |
| Invoice_Overdue     | Daily, on each of `Overdue_Reminder_Days` (1, 7, 14) after the due date | Invoice bill-to                   |
| Waitlist_Offer      | A waitlisted learner is offered a seat                                  | Learner's fee payer               |
| Refund_Paid         | A refund is paid out                                                    | Payee                             |
| Session_Cancelled   | A class session is cancelled                                            | Each enrolled learner's fee payer |

Each template has Subject, Body (with `{{Token}}` merge fields, listed in its description), Active, Send Email and Show in Portal. Amounts are shown as ₹ with two decimals.

## Scheduled jobs

| Job                   | When          | Does                                                                                           |
| --------------------- | ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| KEM Payment Reminders | Daily 09:05   | Due-soon and overdue reminders, then delivers queued email                                     |
| KEM Message Dispatch  | Hourly at :20 | Delivers email queued where a job could not be started (e.g. inside recurring billing batches) |

## User interface

Learner record page → **Messages** (`kemMessages`, sidebar):

- The learner and each guardian with role, fee payer, and email consent, with **Opt out / Allow email**.
- A banner while email delivery is off.
- Filters: All, Email, Portal, Not delivered. Each message shows its subject, channel, recipient, time, sender (staff messages), status and reason; click to read the full text.
- **New message** (`kemMessageModal`): to the learner or a guardian (fee payer preselected), by email and portal / email / portal, with a warning when the email would be suppressed or not sent.
