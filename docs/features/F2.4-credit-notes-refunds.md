# F2.4 — Credit notes and refund automation

**Status:** ✅ Built · ✅ Deployed · ✅ Unit tested · ✅ End-to-end checked (2026-10-05)

## Data model

| Object                                | Purpose                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Credit_Note__c` (CN-000001)          | Money owed back to a payer: origin (Overpayment, Transfer, Withdrawal, Goodwill, Other), amount, applied to invoices, refunded, **balance** (formula), status Open → Partially Used → Used, or Void |
| `Refund__c` (RFD-000001)              | Payout of credit: amount, method, status Requested → Approved → Paid (or Rejected), requester, decider, auto-approved flag, payout reference                                                        |
| `Student_Payment__c`                  | New method **Credit Note** and `Credit_Note__c` lookup: applying credit is recorded as a payment so the allocation ledger stays the single record of what an invoice received                       |
| Custom permission KEM_Approve_Refunds | Administrators and Branch Managers. Finance requests and pays refunds; it cannot approve them                                                                                                       |
| Settings                              | `Refund_Auto_Approve_Limit` (1000), `Auto_Apply_Credit` (true)                                                                                                                                      |

Both objects are private, history-tracked, and their status changes are governed by `Status_Transition__mdt` (triggers). Used credit notes and paid refunds cannot be deleted. The org has reached its custom tab limit, so they have no tabs: they are reached from the Finance Desk, invoices, payments and learners, and through search.

## Where credit comes from

| Source                        | How                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Overpayment                   | Finance Desk → Reconciliation exceptions → **Hold as credit**: the unallocated amount becomes a credit note for the payer and the payment is reconciled with a note naming it |
| Transfer to a cheaper class   | Automatic (F2.2): after the original enrolment was invoiced, the difference is issued as a Transfer credit note linked to the original invoice                                |
| Withdrawal / goodwill / other | Invoice → **Issue credit note** on a Paid or Partially Paid invoice; at most what was paid less earlier credit on that invoice                                                |

## How credit is used

- **Automatically on the next invoice** (`Auto_Apply_Credit`): whenever an invoice is issued, manually or by recurring billing, the payer's open credit is applied oldest first, leaving any amount reserved by pending refunds.
- **By hand** from the credit note: **Apply** on any open invoice of the same payer.
- Each application is a confirmed, reconciled payment with method Credit Note (no cash receipt); it does **not** count as money collected on the Finance Desk or in Insights.

## Refunds

1. Finance requests a refund from the credit note (amount, method, reason). Pending refunds reserve the balance, so it cannot be spent twice.
2. Up to `Refund_Auto_Approve_Limit` it is **approved automatically**; above it, a KEM_Approve_Refunds user **other than the requester** approves or rejects (rejecting frees the reservation).
3. Finance pays it out and records the payout reference; the credit note's refunded amount and status update.

## User interface

- `kemCreditNote` (Credit Note record page): amounts with a usage bar, payer, learner, source invoice/payment, reserved amount, **Request refund**, **Void** (unused only), open invoices with **Apply**, refund timeline with **Approve / Reject / Mark paid**, invoices paid from the credit.
- `kemRefundModal`: amount (defaults to what is available), method, reason, and whether approval will be needed.
- `kemCreditModal` from the invoice: amount (≤ paid), origin, reason; opens the new credit note.
- Finance Desk: **Open credit** KPI, **Refunds** queue (awaiting approval, ready to pay) with the same actions, **Hold as credit** on exceptions. The invoice payments list shows credit as "Credit CN-…".
