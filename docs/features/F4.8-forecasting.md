# F4.8 — Forecasting: enrolments and seats, invoicing and cash

**Status:** ✅ Built · ✅ Deployed · ✅ Unit tested · ✅ End-to-end checked (2026-10-06)

The **Forecasts** panel (`kemForecast`) is on the Home page (all branches, with a branch picker) and on each Branch page. It looks 3 or 6 months ahead, starting with the current month. Everything is read as the user, so people see forecasts only for what they may see: enrolment figures need access to enrolments, finance figures need access to invoices and payments (teachers see neither).

## What it shows

| Part                      | How it is worked out                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New enrolments**        | Up to 24 completed months of new enrolments, forecast by the best of three methods (below), with an 80% range. With under 6 months of history, the forecast is the **admissions pipeline** spread over the months (earliest first; the range runs from none to every pipeline item enrolling)                 |
| **Pipeline**              | Open enquiries, applications in review, offers awaiting a reply and the waitlist, each times its own 12-month enrol rate (enquiry → converted × application → enrolled; offers accepted; waitlist enrolled). Without completed history an assumed rate is used and marked "(assumed)": 30%×70%, 70%, 80%, 50% |
| **Seats**                 | Per course: free seats in Open, Planned and Full classes running in the period, against the forecast new enrolments shared by each course's intake of the last 6 months. **Short** when at least one expected learner has no seat, **Tight** when fewer than 20% (at least one) spare                         |
| **Invoicing**             | Monthly invoiced amounts, forecast the same way. With under 6 months of history: the **monthly fees of current learners** (committed billing); one-time fees of new learners come on top                                                                                                                      |
| **Expected cash**         | Per month: balances due on open invoices (by instalment due date where there is a plan; overdue balances in the current month) plus monthly fees to be billed, times the 12-month collection rate (100% until there is a completed month)                                                                     |
| **Notes**                 | Short history, assumed rates and seat shortfalls, in plain words                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Explain with Einstein** | For KEM AI users: Einstein explains the figures for a manager (outlook, reliability, two or three actions). It is given only the computed figures and told not to calculate or invent; it never changes them. Logged as feature _Forecast Narrative_ with helpful / not helpful feedback                      |

## Methods and accuracy (`ForecastMath`)

Three candidates are **backtested** on the last 3 completed months (fitted without them, then asked to predict them); the lowest error wins:

- **Average** — mean of the last three months (stable demand);
- **Trend** — Holt's linear exponential smoothing, smoothing chosen by the lowest one-step error (growth or decline);
- **Seasonal** — the same month last year scaled by the recent level (from 15 months of history).

Accuracy is the backtest's mean absolute percentage error: **Good** ≤ 15%, **Fair** ≤ 35%, **Low** above; "Not enough history" under 6 months. Leading months before the institution had any data are ignored. The range is ±1.28 × the backtest's error spread × √(months ahead).

## Agentforce

Action **Forecast KEM Enrolments and Revenue** (`AgentForecast`, no generative AI) is part of the Kasetti Education topic: "How many new enrolments should we expect next quarter, and will we have enough seats?" returns the forecast lines with ranges and accuracy for all branches or a named branch. The topic tells the agent to mention the accuracy and that forecasts are estimates.

## Access

`ForecastController` is available to administrators, branch managers, academic coordinators and finance (the analytics personas); the explanation needs **KEM AI User**. Prompt: `Forecast_Narrative` (170 words); evaluation case `Forecast_Narrative_Grounded`.
