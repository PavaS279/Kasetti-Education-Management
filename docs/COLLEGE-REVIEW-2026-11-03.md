## 2. What is solid (enterprise-grade)

- **Admit is atomic.** Seat, enrolment and invoice are created under one savepoint, with the application row locked `FOR UPDATE`, so repeat calls are idempotent ([ProgrammeEnrolmentService.cls](../force-app/main/default/classes/ProgrammeEnrolmentService.cls) 76–160).
- **Interview and decision are enforced on the server.** Each action checks a custom permission, that the record is editable, and the stage ([InterviewService.cls](../force-app/main/default/classes/InterviewService.cls)).
- **Aadhaar handling is correct.** Only the last 4 digits are accepted and stored, and Identity files are deleted ([CollegeDocumentService.cls](../force-app/main/default/classes/CollegeDocumentService.cls) 196–231).
- **Roll numbers are safe.** The sequence row is locked, a retry handles duplicates, the keys are unique, numbers are never reused, and lateral entry starts at 401 ([RollNumberService.cls](../force-app/main/default/classes/RollNumberService.cls)).
- **Cash and DD rules are enforced on the server.** Cash is refused; a DD needs number, bank and date and starts as Pending ([CollegePaymentPolicy.cls](../force-app/main/default/classes/CollegePaymentPolicy.cls)).
- **The public page is hardened for guests.** Inputs are bounded, there is a honeypot and a form-age window, and repeated submissions are idempotent ([PublicEnquiryController.cls](../force-app/main/default/classes/PublicEnquiryController.cls)).
- **Provisional expiry is safe.** It uses a savepoint per record plus `FOR UPDATE` ([CollegeProvisionalService.cls](../force-app/main/default/classes/CollegeProvisionalService.cls)).
